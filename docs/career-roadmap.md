# Career Direction & Roadmap — Intelligent Distribution & Decision Systems

Reference memory for the Lead Intelligence Agent project. Use this whenever we need to connect project decisions to the long-term career thread.

## Why this project matters

The lead intelligence agent is the career foundation:

- **Frontend (customer-facing):** conversation design where the agent captures qualified leads.
- **Backend (human rep-facing):** operational workspace design for agent and human rep collaboration.
- **Pivot point:** intelligent decision routing, which leads into distribution and matching systems.

## Product vision: Intelligent Distribution Systems

**From product creation to intelligent distribution.**

Build intelligent systems that understand real-world needs, discover available capabilities, and make better matching and resource allocation decisions. The right resources reach the right needs, at the right time, under the right conditions.

Driving question:

> How can we use intelligence to ensure that what exists reaches where it is genuinely needed?

This thread connects the Northstar project, AI agents, marketplace liquidity, and resource allocation.

## Capabilities to build

1. **AI product thinking:** design AI systems that interpret needs and make decisions.
2. **Systems thinking:** understand how demand, supply, constraints, and outcomes interact.
3. **Decision intelligence:** translate ambiguous information into explainable decisions.
4. **Optimization thinking:** matching algorithms, operations research, resource allocation.
5. **Platform strategy:** design infrastructure that connects fragmented ecosystems.

**AI Product Designer → Intelligent Systems & Decision Architecture → Distribution Intelligence Product Strategist**
