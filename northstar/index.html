How It Works

Three habits of a good first conversation.

  1. 01

    Understand Intent

    The agent starts with the visitor’s situation, not a form. It asks what brought them here and why it matters, so every follow-up is relevant instead of generic.

  2. 02

    Build Trust

    It says plainly that it is an AI, explains why it is asking each question, and doesn’t press for contact details before there is a reason to share them.

  3. 03

    Qualify Responsibly

    It gathers evidence about process, volume and goals. When something needs verification or a person’s authority, it says so and hands off rather than guessing.

Product Experience

Move beyond the form submission.

A form captures fields. It can’t ask a follow-up, notice hesitation, or tell a sales team what it still needs to confirm. The agent turns the same inbound interest into a conversation that leaves behind something a rep can actually use.

A form submission

  • Fixed fields, answered or skipped
  • Intent inferred from a few dropdowns
  • No way to ask “what did you mean?”
  • Fragmented signals across tools

A qualification conversation

  • One relevant question at a time
  • Needs described in the visitor’s own words
  • Reasons for each question explained
  • Clear next step, or a clear handoff

For sales teams

Evidence over assumptions

What the visitor said about their process and goals stays attached to the lead, so the first human conversation starts further along.

Honest boundaries

The agent doesn’t invent guarantees, pricing, or technical commitments. Anything it can’t establish, it names as unverified.

Handoffs that make sense

Legal, security, and authorization questions go to a person, with an explanation to the visitor of why.
